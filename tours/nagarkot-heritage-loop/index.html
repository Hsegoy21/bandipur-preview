Nagarkot Heritage Loop

Two easy days linking Kathmandu Valley's hilltop viewpoints and heritage towns.

Region
Kathmandu Valley
Duration
2 days
Group size
2–8 riders
Fitness level
Easy

A gentle two-day loop for riders who want culture alongside their cycling. Day one climbs steadily through Bhaktapur’s old town and past Changunarayan to Nagarkot for sunset over the valley; day two loops back through Sankhu and Boudha at an easy pace. Overnight stays and all meals are included, so you can focus on riding rather than logistics.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Kathmandu to Nagarkot

    Ride through Bhaktapur's old town and past Changunarayan, climbing steadily to Nagarkot in time for sunset over the valley.

  2. Day 2: Nagarkot to Kathmandu via Sankhu

    An easy descent through Sankhu and Boudha, finishing back in Kathmandu by early afternoon.

What's included

  • Guide
  • All meals
  • Overnight accommodation
  • Heritage site entry fees
